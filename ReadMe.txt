This Doomenstein has several different new features, including a boss fight. below is a list of all features as well as how to test them:

-------------------------------------------
		WEAPONS 
-------------------------------------------
There are a few new mechanics associated with weapons, which include:
- Reloading/magazine sizes (press R)
- Aiming down scopes (press right mouse click)
- precision hits (Basically a headshot, aim down scopes and you'll see a yellow circle near the head of each enemy. That is their precision hitbox.)
- Aim assist (when you hover over an enemy with your reticle, it will stick a little bit to it and try to keep you looking at that enemy. Most apparent when using a controller.)
- Perks (each weapon has a different 'perk' which gives it a special mechanic or feature, such as healing on kill or increased damage as it is fired.)

There are 5 weapons which the player can use, each with different functionality. 
These weapons can be cycled with the left/right arrow keys, or chosen using number
keys 1-5.

-  Huckleberry, Key 1: This is a submachine gun which fires faster the longer you hold the trigger. 
		       getting a kill with it instantly reloads the magazine.

-  Red Death, Key 2:   This is a pulse rifle which will heal 10 HP whenever you get a kill with it. 
		       Currently this gun is the only way to heal.

-  Fourth Horseman, Key 3: This is a 4-barrel shotgun which deals tremendous damage, and increases 
			   its damage and bullet spread with each consecutive shot.

-  Black Spindle, Key 4: This is a sniper rifles which will automatically reload if you land 3 
			 precision hits in a row.

- Portal Gun, Key 5: Its the portal gun from portal, you know what it does.

-------------------------------------------
		ENEMIES 
-------------------------------------------
There are really only two new enemies, the Dinosaur and the Emperor.
Both of these enemies will shoot projectiles at you, but the Emperor
has more health and will additionally drop an item you can pick up 
to beat the boss. These enemies will only spawn if the boss encounter 
has started.

-------------------------------------------
	      PORTALS/RIFTS 
-------------------------------------------
There are two kinds of portals:

- Portal Gun: these are portals that are created with a portal gun, they connect two different points together. Just shoot the portal gun 
	      at two surfaces and you'll create them (left click for left portal, right click for right). There is currently a bug where 
	      if you fire through this portal, it may crash the game.

- Rift: Rifts are portals which lead to a second 'Rift Map'. Going through them keeps your position, but places you in the other map.
	These can be shot through to hit enemies on the other side, but currently enemies can't see you through the rift. Rifts will spawn 
	once the boss has spawned.

-------------------------------------------
	        BOSS FIGHT 
-------------------------------------------
Currently the boss does not spawn naturally. You need to type 'spawnBoss' into the console to summon him. Once you do, an intro cutscene will 
play and the fight will start.

The Devourer of Gods is the boss. He will fly around the arena and occasionally try to ram at you, dealing a large amount of damage. 
He will also occasionally spawn a rift along his flight path and enter it, switching to the other map.

The boss can be damaged anywhere on its body, but dealing damage at the head is the most effective. In order to hit its head
you need to start a 'Damage Phase'. Below is the steps to beginning one:

STARTING A DAMAGE PHASE:

- Find an 'Emperor' enemy and kill it. These spawn much less than Demons and Dinosaurs, and are very large. Check the rift world to see if they 
  spawn there, as they often get stuck on the terrain. You can alternatively type 'spawnOrb' into the console to summon an orb.

- Pick up the Orb that the Emperor dropped by standing near it and pressing E. This will let you grab it, and you can drop it at any time by 
  pressing E again or switching your weapon.

- Bring the Orb to the Spawn Platform, which is located in the very center of the snowy map. Once there press E to deposit the Orb, which will 
  spawn a Ball.

- Enter the Ball by pressing E. While inside the Ball you will take no damage, so you can use it to heal up with Red Death. Wait in the ball until 
  The boss chooses to charge at you, once his head hits the ball with you inside a damage phase will begin.

- Once the boss interacts with the Ball, it will put it in its mouth and fly around with it, which gives you ample opportunity to shoot its head at 
  close range. Black Spindle and Fourth Horseman are both really good weapons to use in this phase. This will continue for around 20 seconds, and then
  the boss will destroy the ball and launch you towards the center of the map (currently glitched and will send you into space).
